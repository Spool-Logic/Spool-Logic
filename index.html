What it does

Passive 3D printer queue tracking

Monitor queued, active, finished, and failed print workflows from one local command center.

Smart load balancing

Route jobs to the shortest machine queue across your print farm. Balance and move work when queues drift.

Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer linking

Link design files from your PC. Paths show on queue cards. Drag queued jobs into Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer (Chrome/Edge).

3MF and design file import

Upload or drop .3mf / design files, attach them to jobs, and keep the real files on your computer — not on our servers.

Filament slots, inventory, accounting

Track AMS-style slots, shelf stock, low-stock warnings, prices per kg, and consumption after complete or fail.

Cost, profit & lag-rate timeline

Material grams, machine hours, waste multiplier, profit records, and a lag-rate slider for realistic shop calendars.
