# SpoolLogic: Local-First 3D Print Farm Queue & Shop Manager

SpoolLogic is a browser-based, local-first **3D printing** queue and shop manager. Track printer queues, filament inventory, and operational costs. Scale across unlimited machines without storing shop data or proprietary 3D designs in the cloud.

This is 3D printing software — unrelated to automotive turbo / diesel brands of a similar name.

## Automated 3D printer queue & slicer workflow

Manage daily shop workflow from one dashboard. This app tracks and plans — it does not replace your slicer or printer firmware.

* **Passive 3D printer queue tracking:** Monitor queued, active, finished, and failed jobs. You set printer status in the app (running / finished / offline).
* **Smart load balancing:** Route jobs to the shortest machine queue; balance and move work across printers.
* **Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer linking:** Drag queued jobs into Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer after you link files from your PC (Chrome/Edge).
* **3MF and design files:** Upload or drop files, keep them in a folder on your computer, and show the full path on each queue card.
* **Desktop file linking:** Grant folder access or paste a path. Print files stay on your PC.

## 3D print cost calculator & filament inventory

* **Cost calculator without spreadsheets:** Material grams, machine hours, and waste multiplier per job (Settings default plus optional per-job override).
* **Lag-rate timeline:** Stretch calendar time for other tasks on Cost Analytics and Queue stats.
* **Profit & margin tracking:** Enter revenue for a period against computed print costs.
* **Filament accounting & inventory:** Shelf stock, past usage, switch recommendations, low-stock warnings (~100g).
* **Multi-printer slot tracking:** Type, color, and remaining grams across AMS-style slots.

## Browser-based privacy & control

* **Local-first storage:** Queues, printers, filament, and history stay in this browser on this device.
* **Cloud-light architecture:** Login and billing in the cloud; operational shop data is not synced to our servers.
* **No cross-device sync:** A new browser starts empty unless you use the same browser profile (by design).
* **Archive in Settings:** Deleted jobs can be auto-archived and reviewed.
