Privacy Policy

SpoolLogic · Version 2.1 · Last updated August 2026 · Public copy of the in-app policy

1. Overview

SpoolLogic ("we", "us", "the Service") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, what stays on your device, and how account sign-in and payments work. Questions: 3dprintingmanagemastertool@gmail.com.

2. Our Local-First Model

Most of your print-shop data stays on your computer, in your browser — not on our servers. We use the cloud only where needed for account sign-in and subscription billing.

  • In the cloud: email, hashed password, display name, trial/subscription status, Stripe customer identifiers, and records of Terms/Privacy acceptance.
  • On your device (local browser storage): print queue, unlimited printer profiles, filament inventory, settings, linked print file names/paths, and related workspace data.
  • On your computer (optional): when you grant folder access in Chrome or Edge, print files can be saved to a folder you choose on your PC. The app stores the filename and path on each queue job in your browser — files remain on your device.

3. Information We Collect

  • Account information — email, optional display name, and a one-way hashed password stored in Supabase (our cloud database provider).
  • Subscription & billing — trial dates, plan status, and payment-related identifiers from Stripe when you subscribe. We do not store full credit-card numbers.
  • Legal acceptance — whether you accepted the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, plus version and timestamp.
  • Print workspace data — queue entries, printer configs, filament records, cost settings, and linked file names/paths — stored locally in your browser.
  • Support messages — if you email us, we receive what you send.
  • Hosting logs — our host (Streamlit Community Cloud) and infrastructure providers may log IP address, browser type, and request timing for security and reliability. We do not use third-party advertising trackers inside the app.

4. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate you and secure your account
  • Provide queue, costing, filament, dashboard, and file-linking features
  • Manage free trials and paid subscriptions through Stripe
  • Send password-reset and transactional email when SMTP is configured
  • Respond to support requests
  • Fix bugs, prevent abuse, and improve the Service

5. Core Features & Your Data

These features are designed around local storage for shop data:

  • Smart queue — job lists stored locally in your browser.
  • Unlimited printers — printer profiles stored locally; no per-printer cloud fee.
  • All 3D & Design Files — link files from your workflow; save to a folder on your PC via Chrome/Edge; the path appears on each queue job in your browser.
  • Job costs, profit, filament, dashboard — calculated and stored from your local workspace.
  • Local saving — queue, printers, filament, and settings stay in your browser on this device; cloud is sign-in and billing only.

Signing in on a new browser or device does not automatically download your queue.

6. Service Providers We Use

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with providers needed to run the Service:

  • Supabase — secure account storage and authentication bridge
  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription management
  • Streamlit Community Cloud — hosts the web application
  • SMTP email provider — password reset and transactional messages (when enabled)

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users from fraud or abuse.

7. Data Security

Passwords are stored using one-way hashing. Cloud database access is restricted to our application. Local workspace data is protected by your device, browser, and operating system — keep your session secure and log out on shared computers.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Account records remain while your account is active. Local browser data persists until you clear site storage or reset the app. You may request account deletion by contacting us; deleting your account does not automatically erase browser data on devices you have used — clear browser storage separately if needed.

9. Your Choices

  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through Stripe's billing portal (linked in the app)
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing us
  • Decline to create an account (the Service requires an account to use)

10. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. International Users

The Service is operated from the United States. If you access it from elsewhere, your information may be processed in the U.S. and where our service providers operate.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes may require you to review and accept an updated version before continuing to use the Service.

13. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: 3dprintingmanagemastertool@gmail.com
