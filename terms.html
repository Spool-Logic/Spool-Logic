2. What the Service Is

SpoolLogic is a browser-based 3D printing management app for hobbyists, makers, and small print businesses. It helps you organize print queues, manage unlimited printers, track filament, estimate costs, and work with All 3D & Design Files alongside Bambu Studio, Orca, and similar slicers. The Service does not replace your slicer, printer firmware, or manufacturer software.

3. Features Included in Your Plan

Subject to these Terms and technical limitations of your device and browser, paid and trial access includes the core capabilities below.

  • Smart queue — reorder jobs and track queued, active, finished, and failed prints.
  • Unlimited printers — add as many printers as you operate, each with its own queue and load-balancing across your shop.
  • All 3D & Design Files — link files from your workflow; save to a folder on your PC in Chrome or Edge; file paths show on queue cards.
  • Real job costs — material, machine time, and waste estimates per job.
  • Profit & margins — pricing and margin tools for jobs you sell or budget.
  • Filament tracking — loaded slots, inventory, usage, and low-stock awareness.
  • Dashboard — overview of queue status, alerts, and cost snapshots.
  • Local saving — queue, printers, filament, settings, and linked file paths stored in your browser on each device.
