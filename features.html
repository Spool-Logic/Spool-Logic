SpoolLogic feature list

A local-first 3D print shop environment: queues, filament, and costs. Unlimited printers. Shop data stays in your browser.

Start 14-day free trial Full numbered spec

Automated 3D printer queue & slicer workflow

  • Passive 3D printer queue tracking — queued, active, finished, and failed jobs in one place. You mark printer status in the app (running / finished / offline).
  • Smart load balancing — assign to the shortest queue; Balance All Queues and move jobs between machines.
  • Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer linking — drag queued jobs into your slicer (Chrome/Edge) after you link files from your PC.
  • 3MF and design files — upload or drop files, keep them in a folder on your computer, and show the full path on each queue card.
  • Desktop file linking — Grant folder access in Chrome or Edge, or paste a path. Files are not stored on our servers.

Cost calculator & filament inventory

  • Cost calculator without spreadsheets — material grams, machine hours, and waste multiplier per job (global default plus per-job override).
  • Lag-rate timeline — stretch calendar time for “time spent on other tasks” on Cost Analytics and Queue stats.
  • Profit & margin tracking — enter revenue for a period against computed print costs and hours.
  • Filament accounting & inventory — shelf stock, past usage, switch recommendations, and low-stock warnings (~100g).
  • Multi-printer slot tracking — type, color, and remaining grams across AMS-style slots.

Browser-based privacy & control

  • Local-first storage — queue, printers, filament, settings, and history stay in this browser on this device.
  • Cloud-light architecture — we host login and billing only. Operational shop data is not synced to our cloud.
  • No cross-device sync — a different browser or computer starts empty unless you use the same browser profile. By design.
  • Archive in Settings — deleted jobs can be auto-archived and reviewed; you can clear archives. Workspace lives in the browser.
